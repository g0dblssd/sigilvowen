# Photographic PBR materials

All material sets are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and licensed
CC0. The game ships 2K JPG albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.

| Local prefix | Poly Haven asset | Author | Runtime use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `graveyard-cobblestone` | [Cobblestone Floor 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_001) | Rob Tuytel | terrain and weathered wood fallback |
| `crypt-wall` | [Castle Brick 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_brick_01) | Rob Tuytel | walls, ruins and stone props |
| `forged-iron` | [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02) | Rob Tuytel | armor fallback, pylons and mechanisms |

Source maps are used as matched PBR sets. Their normal and roughness maps are
not synthesized from the albedo image.
